# Wolf sample groom

A free sample: a stylized wolf model with its GroomLab groom (guides, graph, painted maps), for
learning GroomLab and testing exports.

**Status:** placeholder. The model and groom files are not published yet. They will go here (or
in a release download) as `wolf.groom`, the meshes and their maps.
