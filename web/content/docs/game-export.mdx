## Cards

Add a **Cards** node (Add Node > Games) at the point in the graph that should be converted to
cards, or run `groomlab.create_cards(groom, lod="mid")`. Cards are placed along the groom's clumps
and shape, and their textures are baked from the groom's own strands.

The node's panel shows the cards' cost and has **Show cards**, **Show textures**, **LOD**,
**Bake Maps**, **Bake Scalp** and the exports. While a bake runs, the panel shows a wait cursor,
greys out its buttons and says what it is baking.

- **LODs:** Hero, Mid and Low set a triangle budget (about 50k, 15k and 4k) and a matching card
  layout. Lower levels use fewer cards of the same size, so the silhouette is preserved.
- **Shading consistency:** in the viewport, cards are lit like the strands (normals, highlight
  and ambient), and each card is tinted with its own strands' color, root to tip, while the
  textures stay shared across cards. The exports carry the same data: the normals, and the tint as
  a vertex color (glTF: `_TINT`).
- **Baked maps:** depth, coverage, tangent, attribute, color and AO, in Unreal's hair card texture
  layout.
- **Stable atlas:** after a bake, cards rebuilt by later edits keep their place in the atlas. A
  node whose maps are out of date shows an orange badge.
- **Bake Scalp** bakes the hair near the skin into the scalp's UVs, with a hair cap mesh, so low
  LODs don't show bare skin between cards.
- Cards are always built from the full groom; the viewport preview density does not affect them.

## Export targets

| Target | Menu | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unreal** cards | Cards node > Export Unreal Cards... (or GroomLab > Export > Unreal Cards...) | `_cards.fbx`, the six maps, the hair cap and a JSON describing the channels |
| **Unreal** strands | GroomLab > Export > Unreal Groom... | Alembic in Unreal's groom schema, with guides for simulation |
| **Unity** cards | GroomLab > Export > Unity Cards... | FBX (Y up, meters), PNG maps and suggested HDRP and URP materials |
| **Unity** strands | GroomLab > Export > Unity Strands (.abc)... | Alembic for Unity's strand hair package |
| **glTF** cards | GroomLab > Export > glTF Cards... | `.glb` with embedded maps, one mesh per LOD |

When several LODs are exported into one folder, file names end in `_hero`, `_mid` or `_low`.

```python
groomlab.create_cards(groom, lod="hero")
groomlab.export_cards_unreal(groom, "C:/game/hair", "hair", lod="hero")
groomlab.export_cards_gltf(groom, lods=["hero", "mid", "low"])
groomlab.export_unreal_groom(groom, "C:/game/hair.abc")
```

### Unreal groom attributes

The Unreal groom export writes the attributes Unreal's groom importer reads, as arbGeomParams:
`groom_version_major` / `groom_version_minor`, `groom_group_name`, `groom_group_id` (from the
`groupId` attribute), `groom_id`, `groom_width` (per point), `groom_root_uv` (with scalp UVs),
`groom_color` (per point, with a color node: the hair's appearance, what it looks like, see
[Base color and appearance](/docs/rendering#base-color-and-appearance)), `groom_roughness` (with HairColor's roughness),
`groom_clump_id` (with a Clump), `groom_iridescence` (with FeatherColor iridescence) and
`groom_guide` on the guide curves. It also writes the 64-bit strand ID as
`groomlab_strand_id_low` / `groomlab_strand_id_high`. Other GroomLab attributes, including
Attribute node output, are not written to the Unreal groom file.

## Color patterns in engines

Bands, FeatherColor patterns and HairColor tips are per-point colors, and FeatherColor's
iridescence is a per-point mask ([Color patterns](/docs/color-patterns)):

| Target | Colors | Iridescence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unreal strands | `groom_color` per vertex | `groom_iridescence` per vertex; with **Iridescence as AO** (`iridescence_as_ao=True`) also `groom_ao`, which the Hair Attributes material node exposes |
| Cards (Unreal, glTF, Unity) | Baked into the Color map as the hair's appearance (a feather's card is its vane laid flat, so its patterns map directly) | The Color map's alpha (Unreal; RGBA only then); Unity's Mask map red channel; glTF: not carried |
| Unity strands | One color per strand (`Cd`, as in GroomLab's Alembic; bands and patterns along a strand are not carried) | Not carried |
| glTF cards | The baked `_BaseColor` and each card's `_TINT` | Not carried (the alpha is the coverage) |

The repository's `docs/UNREAL_HAIR_MATERIAL.md` describes an Unreal material (for strands and
cards) that mixes in the iridescent color by that mask, with the hue rotated by the view angle;
`tools/unreal/groomlab_hair_material.json` describes the same graph node by node.

## Feathers in engines

- **As cards:** `groomlab.create_feather_cards(groom, lod="mid")` creates one alpha-tested strip
  per feather, with both vanes and the rachis baked into its texture, and similar feathers
  sharing a few textures. Each card is folded along a spine so lifted or cupped vanes form a V.
  Unreal, Unity and glTF export them.
- **As strands:** the rachis becomes a mesh to skin with the character, and the barbs are strands
  rooted on it.
  - Unreal: `groomlab.create_rachis_mesh`, `export_rachis` and `export_unreal_groom`.
  - Unity: **GroomLab > Export > Unity Feathers...** writes the strands, the rachis FBX and a
    manifest of the Hair Asset settings.
